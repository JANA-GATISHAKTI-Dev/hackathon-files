JANA-GATISHAKTI (जन-गतिशक्ति) is a sovereign Digital Public Good (DPG) designed to solve the fatal flaw in public infrastructure: Governments spend ₹14.5 Lakh Crore on capital works, yet rural and tribal citizens have no seamless, vernacular channel to report hyper-local infrastructure deficits. Jana-GatiShakti aggregates citizen voice across Marathi, Hindi, WhatsApp, and IVR, scrubs PII via zero-knowledge Verhoeff algorithms, clusters demand via ST-HDBSCAN & H3 Hexagons, and formulates mathematically optimized, tenderable capital project dossiers with closed-loop cryptographic accountability.

1. Vernacular Voice & WhatsApp Rail

Eliminates digital divide barriers. A tribal farmer in Gadchiroli simply speaks Marathi on WhatsApp or an IVR call. Two-pass Verhoeff checksum validation cleanses 12-digit Aadhaar & phone numbers before any LLM inference, ensuring strict compliance with the DPDP Act 2023.

📍

2. Authoritative LGD Gazetteer & H3 Hexagons

Resolves vernacular place names against official Local Government Directory (LGD) codes. Clusters raw requests using ST-HDBSCAN with Haversine distance and indexes them into Uber H3 Resolution 8 spatial cells (~0.7 km²) to surface systemic infrastructure deficits.

⚖️

3. Mathematical MILP Knapsack Optimizer

Unlike arbitrary LLM suggestions, capital portfolio selection uses Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (PuLP-CBC) to maximize public welfare subject to hard constraints: ≥40% funding for NITI Aayog Aspirational Districts, ≥30% for SC/ST beneficiaries, and ≤35% single-sector caps.

🏛️

4. Bankable OCDS 1.1 Project Dossiers

Converts citizen complaints directly into tenderable Preliminary Project Reports (PPR) aligned with Jal Jeevan Mission, PMGSY, and PM-KUSUM. Automatically generates and exports standardized Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS 1.1) JSON releases.

🔍

5. Jan-Praman "Ghost Asset" Sentinel

Closes the governance loop. Executes automated post-completion IVR verification calls to citizens, flagging contractors who falsely claim 100% completion when assets remain defective on the ground. Prevents fraudulent capital milestone payouts.

🛡️

6. Cryptographic Audit Ledger & ABAC

Every citizen intake, field verification, and capex sanction is committed to a CERT-In 2022 compliant SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledger with daily Merkle roots. Enforces Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) district scoping and 4-Eyes approval.

1. Tagline & Elevator Pitch (1-2 sentences)

JANA-GATISHAKTI is a sovereign Digital Public Good (DPG) that aggregates vernacular citizen feedback across voice, WhatsApp, and text, verifies demand against official Local Government Directory (LGD) datasets, and synthesizes mathematically optimized, tenderable capital infrastructure capex projects with closed-loop cryptographic accountability.

2. Project Summary / Abstract (~200 Words)

Public capital expenditure often fails to address critical hyper-local infrastructure gaps because citizen feedback remains siloed in fragmented grievance portals, excluded by linguistic barriers, and disconnected from capex planning. JANA-GATISHAKTI bridges this gap as a Digital Public Good (DPG). Citizens report local infrastructure crises (water supply, roads, electricity, healthcare) in vernacular languages (Marathi, Hindi, English) via an official WhatsApp bot or voice IVR. A zero-knowledge pipeline featuring the Verhoeff algorithm cleanses Aadhaar and phone numbers under India’s DPDP Act 2023. Spatial engines apply ST-HDBSCAN and H3 Hexagonal indexing against canonical Local Government Directory (LGD) gazetteers to surface verified demand clusters. Instead of subjective AI suggestions, a Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) knapsack solver formulates bankable project dossiers subject to statutory equity constraints (≥40% Aspirational Districts, ≥30% SC/ST). Post-completion, an automated IVR verification sentinel (Jan-Praman) cross-examines contractor claims against ground citizen audits. The entire lifecycle is anchored in a CERT-In 2022 compliant SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledger with OCDS 1.1 JSON exports, providing policymakers with an end-to-end sovereign DPI rail.

3. Technical Architecture & Sovereign Stack

• Core Architecture: Digital Public Good (DPG) compliant with DPGA 9/9 indicators & Apache 2.0 license. • Edge Ingestion: WhatsApp Bot Simulator + Web Speech API (Marathi mr-IN, Hindi hi-IN, English en-IN). • Privacy Engine: Two-Pass Zero-Knowledge PII Redaction with full Verhoeff Algorithm Aadhaar validation (DPDP Act 2023). • Geo-Spatial Intelligence: ST-HDBSCAN Spatio-Temporal Clustering + Uber H3 Resolution 8 Spatial Indexing (~0.7 km²) + Canonical Local Government Directory (LGD) Gazetteer. • Optimization: Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) knapsack optimization using PuLP-CBC with Aspirational District equity floor (α≥40%) and SC/ST target (β≥30%). • Audit & Governance: CERT-In 2022 compliant SHA-256 hash-chained immutable audit ledger with daily Merkle roots, ABAC district scoping, and 4-Eyes project sanctioning. • Open Standards: OGC GeoJSON + Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS 1.1) + Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool servers. • Map Engine: Zero-API-Key Esri World Dark Gray Canvas, OpenStreetMap, and Esri Satellite.

4. Scalability Across BRICS Nations

Jana-GatiShakti's modular sovereign data layer separates administrative hierarchies from the core analytics engine: • India (Anchor Pilot): Local Government Directory (LGD State 27 Maharashtra, Gadchiroli 990001, Nandurbar 990002, Washim 990003), PMGSY, Jal Jeevan Mission, PM-KUSUM. • Brazil: IBGE 7-digit municipal codes, CPF Modulo-11 sanitization, Portuguese NLP, Sertão semi-arid water infrastructure. • South Africa: Municipal Demarcation Board (MDB) codes, South Africa ID Luhn algorithm check, isiZulu & isiXhosa NLP, Eastern Cape rural clinics. All partner implementations share the exact same OCDS 1.1 capital formulation pipeline and cryptographic audit ledger.
